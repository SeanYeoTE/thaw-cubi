Frequently asked questions

When does a want thaw?

On your next payday, at 9:00 am. Wants under your quick-thaw price use a short timer instead (2 days by default). You can change both in Settings.

My payday isn't on a fixed date. Can I still use Thaw?

Yes. In Settings › Payday, pick Same date every month, Last working day, Twice a month (the 15th and the last day), or No fixed payday, which uses a set wait instead.

What do Keep, Wait and Let go do?

Keep means you've decided to buy it. Wait freezes it until the following payday. Let go removes it from your list and adds its price to your saved total in Stats.

How do I add something from Safari?

On the product page, tap Share, then Thaw. The name is filled in from the page; add the price and your reason. If Thaw isn't in the list, tap More and turn it on.

How do I edit or delete a want?

On Cooling, touch and hold a row for Edit or Delete, or open the want and tap Edit. In Stats, swipe a row. Decided wants can be moved back to cooling from the edit screen.

I'm not getting payday reminders.

Open the iOS Settings app › Notifications › Thaw and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.

Does Thaw sync between devices?

Not yet. Your data is kept only on your iPhone. It moves to a new phone when you restore from a backup.
