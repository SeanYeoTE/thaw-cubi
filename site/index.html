How it works

Most wants melt on their own. Give them time.

1

Freeze it

Add the name, the price and why you want it, in your own words. Browsing in Safari? Share the page to Thaw and the name fills itself in.

2

Let it cool

Your want waits until payday, when you can actually afford it. Small things get a short quick-thaw timer instead, so a phone case doesn't wait a month.

3

Decide on payday

Go through what thawed: Keep, Wait one more payday, or Let go. A meter shows how each choice sits against your fun money.

Meet Cubi

A small ice cube who keeps you company.

Cubi's mood follows your wants and your streak. No guilt, no lectures, just a nudge at the right moment.

Proud

Snoozing

Features

Built around how you actually get paid.

Your payday, your rule

Same date every month, last working day, twice a month, or a set wait if your pay moves around.

Fun money budget

Set what you're happy to spend each payday. The review shows when your keeps go over.

Quick thaws

Wants under a price you choose wait a few days instead of a whole pay cycle.

Share from Safari

Tap Share on any product page. Thaw keeps the link so you can find it again on payday.

See what you saved

A running total of everything you let go, monthly bars, your let-go rate and your payday streak.

Payday reminders

A gentle nudge when wants thaw, or “Remind me tonight” if now isn't the moment.

Made for everyone

Full Dynamic Type, VoiceOver actions on every row, and Cubi rests when Reduce Motion is on.

Private by design

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your wishlist never leaves your phone.
