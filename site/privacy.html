Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

In short: Thaw does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What Thaw stores

The wants you add (name, price, your reason and any link you shared), your decisions, your payday rule, fun money and quick-thaw settings. These are saved in the app's own storage on your device. If you share a page from Safari, the page title and address are passed to Thaw on your device only.

What Thaw does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No servers: Thaw never sends your data over the internet.
  • No sharing or selling of data to third parties.

Notifications

Payday reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. Thaw asks for permission the first time you add a want or save your settings, never at launch. You can turn them off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Backups and deleting your data

Your Thaw data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, under Apple's terms. You can delete any want inside the app, or delete everything by removing Thaw from your iPhone.

Children

Thaw collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Email tseensean@gmail.com.
